Approach

Clarity over noise.

Prefer calm hierarchy, readable type, and interfaces that explain themselves. Accent is used sparingly. Motion stays subtle. The goal is confidence — for the user and for the team shipping next to you.

“Ship the system, then the story.”

— MetaUSI
